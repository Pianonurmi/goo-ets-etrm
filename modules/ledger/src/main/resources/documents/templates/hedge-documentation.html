Hypothetical derivative: a forward on the hedged item at {{designationPrice}} {{currency}} per unit, the official forward price on the designation date, valued like the instruments (official curves, discounted to the expected date). On every official valuation the hedging reserve takes the lower, in absolute terms, of the instruments' change in value since designation ({{baseline}} {{baseCurrency}} then) and the hypothetical derivative's value; the rest is ineffectiveness in profit or loss.
