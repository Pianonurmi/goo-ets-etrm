# Allocation engine

1. **Snapshot**: live `HELD` lots of the entity, minus quantities soft-reserved by other proposals, grouped into lot classes.
2. **Obligations**: open sales (from confirmed trades), intercompany deliveries, disclosure and own-consumption cancellations, each with criteria bound for its delivery date, priority class and optional share constraints (`maxSharePerDevice`, `minShare`).
3. **Eligibility**: class matches criteria, expires after the delivery date, and a feasible transfer route exists to the target registry.
4. **Value and cost** (ADR 0035), in EUR/MWh: a class is worth the best price it qualifies for, the highest official vintage mark over every bucket it satisfies. A class expiring within 60 days of delivery (`etrm.allocation.value.liquidity-horizon-days`) keeps `days / 60` of that value. Cost = value − expiry haircut + transfer fee; lower is better. Give-away = value − the mark of the sale's own bucket at its vintage, reported but not added to the cost. Scarcity (sum over competing obligations of demand / eligible supply) only breaks ties between classes of equal cost. A class with no priced bucket is worth zero and flagged unpriced.
5. **Greedy solve**: obligations ordered by eligible supply / demand, priority, delivery date, specificity; classes by score. Min-share quotas first, device caps as running limits. Deterministic. Scores depend on the class, the delivery date and the target registry only, so classes are ranked once per (date, registry) and each obligation walks that ranking.
6. **Coverage**: Dinic max-flow augmented per priority tier on the compressed graph (ADR 0036: obligations with identical eligible supply and supply with identical demand merge, exactly) → covered / shortfall per obligation (a merged group's flow goes by delivery date, then id), shortage groups (connected components of exhausted supply), surplus by bucket and production year. The same core answers headroom: how much more a new demand could get without taking coverage from anyone.
7. **Value** (`PlanValue`, on what-ifs and plans): total cost, delivered value, expiry haircut, transfer fees, give-away and unpriced MWh, with the figures per obligation sorted by give-away. Plans scored before ADR 0035 (`unit = SCORE`) have none.
8. **Shadow prices** (`PlanPricing`, ADR 0035): a GLOP linear program delivers each obligation's planned coverage at the lowest cost under the same limits. Its duals give the marginal cost per obligation and the scarcity rent per class (reported per bucket and production year); its cost bounds the plan, and the gap is what better lot choices could still save. Indicative, never booked; skipped above `etrm.allocation.pricing.max-pairs` with the reason.
9. **Available to sell** (`HeadroomQuery`, ADR 0036): the most a new sale could receive from held lots without taking anything from existing obligations (rerouting allowed), per delivery and for a trade's deliveries together, with the exhausted supply that limits it. Prepared once per entity (live lots, open obligations less what approved plans have reserved; about 3 s to build at design size, then patched in place from the changed lots and obligations in about 0.3 s, ADR 0043) and checked against a stamp on every read (a check takes about 0.5 ms); a table by bucket, vintage and month-end; a `GOO_HEADROOM` pre-trade finding that never blocks capture.
10. **Portfolio** (`PortfolioQuery`, ADR 0037): the bucket hierarchy (proven containment, aliases merged, smallest containers as parents) with, per bucket, long, short (obligations only its certificates can serve), covered within (max-flow inside the bucket), short after netting, naive net, free and available to sell, plus the mark, the cheapest to deliver and the give-away if sold now (management view).
11. **Future supply** (ADR 0038): purchases (open quantity of GoO BUY schedules, eligible by implication) and expected issuance of own devices (forecast or capacity × load factor, less what is issued, after the issuance lag) enter the problem as stand-in lot classes, available from their date. Plans count on them at P90 with virtual lines, never reserved or instructed; a change to the purchase, the forecast or an issuance makes a proposed plan stale and marks an approved plan's virtual lines CHANGED. Available to sell, the portfolio view and coverage report by tier (held, + contracted, + expected, at P50).
11. **Plan**: lines at lot level (earliest expiry first, last lot split), `PROPOSED` with soft reservations (default 48 h), four-eyes `approve` under a per-entity advisory lock → hard reservations (`RESERVED` lots, splits) → registry instructions → confirmations fulfil obligations and record trade deliveries → `CONFIRMED` or `PARTIALLY_FAILED`. Lots that can no longer be reserved mark the plan `STALE`.

Exact solver (`solver: EXACT`, ADR 0031): OR-Tools CP-SAT over the same lot classes and eligibility. It solves three
objectives in order: most MWh covered, one priority tier at a time from the highest (ADR 0035), least `MinShare`
shortfall, then lowest total cost. Class supply, demand and
`MaxSharePerDevice` are hard limits. It starts from the greedy solution and is never worse than greedy: the two are compared on coverage per priority tier, then shortfall, then cost (ADRs 0035, 0036).
Greedy's result is used instead, with the reason in the plan's `objective.fallbackReason`, when the problem has more
than `etrm.allocation.exact.max-pairs` pairs, when no solution is found within `etrm.allocation.exact.time-limit`
(10 s), or when the native library cannot load. `objective.solver` says which solver ran, and `objective.status`
whether CP-SAT proved the result optimal.

Invariants (property-tested): never over-allocate a class, only eligible classes, never over-fill an obligation, device caps hold, greedy allocation ≤ max-flow coverage, same input → identical plan; the compressed flow agrees with the uncompressed one (totals per tier, minimal cut, shortage groups); headroom h fits and h + 1 does not; ranking per score key gives exactly the per-obligation greedy result; a generic sale takes generic supply while a specific sale keeps its certificates; the plan's value adds up from its lines; joint headroom fits and one more MWh does not (brute force); shadow prices satisfy LP duality (non-negative reduced costs, zero where the LP delivers, dual objective = primal), and an optimal exact plan has no gap.

Scale at 100 000 lots × 5 000 obligations (October 2026): `AllocationBenchmarkTest` (lots that outlive every delivery) greedy 6 s, coverage 0.7 s, compressed flow 21 groups × 12 atoms solved in 0.2 s, one headroom check about 1 ms; `PortfolioPrepareBenchmarkTest` (a year of expiry dates, three target registries) problem build 1.3 s (criteria once per distinct bound criterion, expiry and routes as masks), compressed flow 2 249 groups × 968 atoms built in 0.7 s (by profile) with 19 500 edges once nested eligibility is chained, so an entity's figures are prepared in about 3 s and patched in about 0.3 s; a check takes about 30 ms at the 95th percentile and the 192-cell table about 4 s.
