Provena · 16 · Every increment ends green: full build, end-to-end tests, image scan, then main

The system was built in increments that each ended in a working, tested release, tagged since 0.9.0. The first three built the domain and the back office without a screen; four and five put every area on the screen; six made it operable at scale; seven made it demonstrable; eight to ten turned the GoO book into a managed portfolio with figures fast enough for a production-size book; eleven to thirteen deepened valuation and added cleared futures and options on them. In October 2026 an independent review graded the system against enterprise expectations; increments fourteen to seventeen acted on every code finding: the authentication and authorisation gaps, the accounting defects, sealed and verified hash chains, alerting and runbooks, a point-in-time recovery drill, asynchronous long runs, a circuit for registry outages, and tests that run again on the same database. Each increment has its plan, its milestone list and its decision records in the repository.
