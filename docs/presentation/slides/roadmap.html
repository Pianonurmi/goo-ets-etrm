Provena · 16 · Every increment ends green: full build, end-to-end tests, image scan, then main

The system was built in increments that each ended in a working, tested release. The first three built the domain and the back office without a screen; four and five put every area on the screen; six made it operable at scale; seven made it demonstrable; eight turned the GoO book into a managed portfolio; nine is housekeeping and this documentation; ten made the portfolio figures fast enough for a production-size book. Each increment has its plan, its milestone list and its decision records in the repository.
