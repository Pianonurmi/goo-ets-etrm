# EU ETS compliance

For each installation: verified emissions, free allocation and the allowances to surrender by the deadline.

The page shows one compliance year; change it in the page header. It opens with four cards: the net allowance
position, emissions (best estimate, and the verified figure once there is one), free allocation received, and the
next deadline. Below them are the compliance table by installation, a chart of allowances held against emissions,
and the deadlines. Click an installation to open it. Its page repeats the key figures and has three tabs:

- **Emissions:** monthly estimates (**Record estimate**) and verified emissions (**Record verified emissions**);
  verified emissions need a second person.
- **Free allocation** per year (**Set free allocation**).
- **Surrenders** are requested from the installation (**Request surrender**) and sent as Union Registry
  instructions.

The **Installation register** at the bottom of the compliance page lists installations with their permits;
**Register installation** adds one.

## Deadlines

The compliance position compares allowances held with emissions to cover. Deadlines show on the page and, under
**Coming up**, on the home page of the people who act on them.
